### 🔬 Molecular Targets (SNPs of Interest)
My computational process focuses on the analysis of specific variants that dictate how an individual processes pain medication.
<img width="1746" height="984" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/adad8770-b997-450d-a535-b4efc1a27cd5" />

**Key Targets Analyzed:**
* **CYP2C9 (rs1799853 & rs1057910):** Essential for NSAID metabolism.
* **COMT (rs4680):** Associated with pain threshold and sensitivity.
* **PTGS2 (rs20417):** Predicts COX-2 inhibitor effectiveness.
